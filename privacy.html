Privacy

Eat What? has no account or app server. Saved places, meals, areas, preferences and recently shown shop IDs stay in this browser's local storage. They do not sync automatically. You can export and import a JSON backup in My food.

When you request an Eat out suggestion, the area you typed is sent to Photon to find nearby mapped shops. Your approximate coordinates are sent instead only when you explicitly choose Near me. The selected shop's public map ID is sent to the OpenStreetMap API for listed details. If that shop's map record links to a Wikimedia Commons photo, the app requests its thumbnail and credit from Wikimedia Commons. These services have their own privacy practices. Google Maps opens only when you tap its link; Google's privacy policy then applies. The optional font service may receive a request when you are online. No Google Maps API key is used.
